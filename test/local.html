Sample subtitle files

Two files sit next to this page in test/. Every cue announces the timecode it should appear at, so you can check it against the clock burned into the clip.

  • sample.srt — correctly timed. Cue n should appear exactly when the clip's clock reads the time printed in the cue.
  • sample-drift.srt — the same lines, but authored at the wrong framerate (25 vs 23.976). It starts almost right and slips further behind as the clip runs, which is what the Drift section of the Sync tab is for.

What to check

  1. Press Make 100s clip and wait for it. The panel should appear over the video once it is a film-length clip (the 10s one is ignored on purpose).
  2. File tab: load sample.srt. Subtitles should match the burned-in clock, and the panel should jump to the Sync tab with a "Loaded 20 lines" toast that disappears by itself.
  3. Sync tab, player controls: drag the seek bar and the video follows; play/pause toggles; the back/forward buttons skip by the number in the box (1s by default, try 5 and 0.5). Lines and Drift are on by default and Timing is off; each toggle shows and hides its section.
  4. Turn Timing on and press +0.5, or nudge with G/H: the subtitles move and a toast says so. Then click the line you can see in the list; it should snap back into place.
  5. Load sample-drift.srt. In Drift, set anchor A on an early line and anchor B on a late one (each time click the anchor, then the line you hear), and press Apply rate fix. A toast should offer Snap to 23.976 → 25.
  6. Find tab: the dot at the bottom should turn green ("working") within a few seconds. It runs a real search/download test, at most once every 24 hours. Search for a film, or use Series to pick a show, a season and an episode (the single row with ‹ and › runs the search for you). Needs internet.
  7. Style tab: change a preset and a slider; the subtitles update live.
  8. Go fullscreen. Both the subtitles and the panel must stay visible.
  9. In the Sites tab, switch localhost off. The panel and the subtitles should vanish immediately, with no reload. Turn it back on from the toolbar popup and they should come straight back.
