Sample subtitle files

Two files sit next to this page in test/. Every cue announces the timecode it should appear at, so you can check it against the clock burned into the clip.

  • sample.srt — correctly timed. Cue n should appear exactly when the clip's clock reads the time printed in the cue.
  • sample-drift.srt — the same lines, but authored at the wrong framerate (25 vs 23.976). It starts almost right and slips further behind as the clip runs, which is what Fix drift in the Sync tab is for.

What to check

  1. Press Make 100s clip and wait for it, then play it.
  2. The panel should appear over the video. Load sample.srt — subtitles should match the burned-in clock.
  3. Nudge the timing off with G, then click the line you can see in the Sync tab. It should snap back into place.
  4. Load sample-drift.srt. Set anchor A on an early line and anchor B on a late one, press Apply rate fix, and it should offer to snap to 23.976 → 25.
  5. Go fullscreen. Both the subtitles and the panel must stay visible.
  6. In the Sites tab, switch localhost off. The panel and the subtitles should vanish immediately, with no reload. Turn it back on from the toolbar popup and they should come straight back.
